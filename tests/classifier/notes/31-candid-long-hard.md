# Week of 10 March

Harbor Mill ships two jobs this week. Birch Lane Market takes the crate order on Tuesday. The hotel site takes the sideboard sample on Thursday if the finish is dry.

Fire the kiln at 6 a.m. on Thursday. Alex Morrow runs the floor. The saw belt is due Wednesday. Until it lands, the short runs stay on the backup saw.

North Lot School asked for a quote on 40 more crates. Send it by Thursday. They have not committed. The quote is a customer price, about $1,400 with the heavier corner. Remind them to pay the invoice on order 1881 before we cut the new blanks.

Pinebox Supply can deliver corners in 10 days at $18 a crate equivalent. That is a vendor price. Accept it if the count stays at 40.

The spring show booth map is with the printer. Three price cards use last year's type and need a redo. Doors open at 9 and close at 4. Riley Voss books the booth table. That is a front-desk task.

The owner avoids hard calls, so the hotel date may stall even if the shop is ready.

Retain the sample board for 30 days after the Friday walkthrough, then put it back on the rack. Also retain the school deposit check until that order ships. The deposit is a customer payment on account.

Thursday's route goes to Birch Lane Market first, then the school, then the hotel site. The school dock closes at 2. Tell the driver on Wednesday.

Inventory on Monday: 140 oak blanks, 60 crate corners, 9 cans of oil. Reorder oak at 80. No rush this week.

The website page stays a draft until 18 April. Two sideboard frames need a reshoot because the lamp was in the shot. The photographer is Tuesday morning.

Close the register the usual way. If the drawer is off by more than $20, leave a note for the bookkeeper and do not force the slip.

Eye protection stays on at every saw. Spares are in the blue bin. That is a station rule.

No new product joins the spring list after Friday. The open packer role stays posted on the door.
