# Shop week, payroll Friday

Monday 6 April. Harbor Mill. The week is crates, the inn rail, and a library cart that is still a repair. This note is the office copy of the board.

- Customer checks go in the safe, not in the vendor tray.
- A visitor who will not wear glasses does not walk the floor. That is a station rule.
- Driver copies the card onto the clipboard. The office copy stays on the nail.
- Birch Lane Market's crate day is the standing Tuesday slot unless the card says otherwise.
- Crate corners reorder at 40. Pinebox Supply is the vendor. Lead time has been ten days.
- The hotel site and the inn are different jobs. Do not share hardware between the two carts.
- Do not pull crate corners for a sample. The sample uses the already-finished board in the loft.
- Show doors, when the show happens, open at 9 and close at 4.

Clock and money words show up below because Friday is a paperwork day. None of it is a person's wage, and none of it is a judgment of anyone's work.

Payroll run is Friday. The bookkeeper takes the crew clock sheet that morning and the vendor tray with it. The sheet is hours on the shop clock, totals by day, not a line for any one person and not a review.

Pay the vendor invoice stack the same morning if the totals match the packing slips. Pinebox invoice 441 is in the tray: hinges and corners, a vendor bill. Lowood's oak bill is beside it. If a slip is missing, the bill waits. It does not get guessed.

Someone wrote "hire a subcontractor for the job" on the rail card as a maybe. No shop was called. No person was named. It stays a maybe until the rail date is real. The performance of the new pump is on a separate card: gauge in the green before sanding, housing stays closed. That performance is the machine.

Red Kettle Cafe is a customer for bread boards, not a second company we keep books for. Their buyer asked for a darker oil on the next six. Customer price for those six, if they sign, is $420. They have not signed. Fen Coast Inn is the rail job. Quill and Brass Print is only the printer for the three price cards.

Half sentences from the pad:
- payroll run is Friday. sheet on the nail Wednesday night
- vendor tray, not the safe
- cafe order unsigned
- pump gauge, green or stop
- subcontractor line is a maybe. no call yet
- school dock still 2
- do not mix the oil lots while counting the clock sheet

The owner asked, in the doorway, that the Tuesday crate truck leave on time. That is a date he wants. The shop can hit it if the corners on the rack are the corners on the card.

- Sable Oil lots do not get mixed in one can. The new can stays sealed until the old one is empty.
- Scrap oak goes in the scrap bin, not back on the blank count.
- Dust hose on the backup saw was taped last month. Look at the tape before the first cut.
- The front desk books the booth table. That is a calendar task.
- Phone messages go on the pad, with the time, even if the call felt short.
- The pad from yesterday is still under the new pad. Look there before saying no one called.
- The nail test is the finisher's call on the day. Write the result on the board.
- The sample board leans on the north wall. It is not on the ship list.

Close the register Thursday night the usual way so Friday morning is the sheet and the tray, not a drawer hunt. Eye protection rule is unchanged.
