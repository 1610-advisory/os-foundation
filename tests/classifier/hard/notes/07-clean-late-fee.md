# Customer late fee, school balance

Tuesday 14 April. Harbor Mill. North Lot School still owes on the crate invoice. The work shipped. The paper did not come back.

- Soft-close hinges for the inn job are a one-week lead from Pinebox Supply.
- If the finish room feels damp, the kiln goes on at 6 a.m. and the north window opens a finger.
- Lowood delivers on their route, not on a called pickup, unless the board says a pickup was booked.
- A visitor who will not wear glasses does not walk the floor. That is a station rule.
- Labels on the carts face the aisle. A cart with the label in was loaded wrong last week.
- The pack bench light flickers on the left tube. Leave it until Friday's electrician window.
- Milltown Library's cart is a repair, not a new build, until the broken wheel is in the shop.
- On a dry day the kiln stays off. The oil wants the slow cure.

A late fee here is money a customer owes Harbor Mill. It is not a bill we send to ourselves.

Pay the invoice reminder, worded plain:

Invoice 1904, North Lot School, crates, $1,280, shipped complete. Due last Friday. If a Harbor Mill customer does not pay the invoice within 30 days, add a late fee of 1.5% of the open balance each month. The 30 days are not up yet. The note goes out now so the due date is in their hands. No late fee added today.

Pasted draft of the reminder:

Your crate order shipped complete. Invoice 1904 is $1,280. Please pay the invoice by the due date on the paper. A late fee applies only after 30 days, at 1.5% of what is still open. The next order is not cut until this one is paid. Questions on the count go to the shop phone.
