- Crate corners reorder at 40. Pinebox Supply is the vendor. Lead time has been ten days.
- Lowood Lumber's sticker on the oak still says the March lot, and the count on the sticker does not match the rack.
- Driver copies the card onto the clipboard. The office copy stays on the nail.
- A visitor who will not wear glasses does not walk the floor. That is a station rule.
- Eye protection stays on at every saw. Spares live in the blue bin by the north door.
- Black pulls get reordered at ten. Hart and Coil is the vendor. The price is a material cost.
- Do not pull crate corners for a sample. The sample uses the already-finished board in the loft.

Our hauler is not the interesting part. One name in the mail is.

Our plan, unchanged: our truck, our rail, Fen Coast Inn side door 9 to 11, pads, chip, closed oil can, no desks.

Subject: pallet

Do not write that pallet into the inn job. Do not let it delay the 7:30 leave.
