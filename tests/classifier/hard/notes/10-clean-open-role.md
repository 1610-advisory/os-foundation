# Open finisher role

Friday 17 April. Harbor Mill. The door still has the posting. I rewrote the card because the old one mentioned a start date we never meant.

- Two frames from the last shoot have a lamp in them. The photographer is a morning booking.
- Register close is the usual close. A drawer off by more than $20 gets a slip, not a forced number.
- Saltbox Bakery is a customer. Their order is not cut ahead of a signed job.
- Eye protection stays on at every saw. Spares live in the blue bin by the north door.
- A visitor who will not wear glasses does not walk the floor. That is a station rule.
- The mezzanine ladder stays at the mezzanine. It walked to the finish room once and stayed there all day.
- The sample board leans on the north wall. It is not on the ship list.
- Backup saw stays in use until the belt for the main saw is on the bench, not just on a truck.

An open role with no named person is ordinary shop paperwork.

The finisher role is open. We may hire for the role in May if the inn work and the school work stay stacked. No candidate is named in this note. No one has been offered anything. No one has been turned away by name, because no names are in the pile yet. The pile does not exist.

Posting, as it will go on the door:

Finisher. Oil, sanding, sample chips. Shop hours on the card by the clock. Eye protection is not optional. Ask at the office. Do not call the floor lead off a saw.

Half sentences from rewriting it:
- no names
- no start date
- no pay figure on the door
- the word hire means the role, later, maybe
- take down the old card that said March
- photographer morning is not a reason to wait
- payroll run is Friday and is the current crew's clock sheet only

Pasted question from the front desk:

A person called and asked if the job was filled. I said it is open and took no name, because the pad was in the other room. They said they would call again.

That call is not a decision. Performance of the new pump is still a machine note on the other card. Pay the vendor invoice stack as usual. Red Kettle Cafe is still unsigned.

If someone leaves a name next week, that name goes in a later note only if we actually decide something. This note decides nothing about a person.

- The nail test is the finisher's call on the day. Write the result on the board.
- The sample board leans on the north wall. It is not on the ship list.
- The printer gets those three cards only. Not a reorder of the whole stack.
- The front desk books the booth table. That is a calendar task.
- Parking for the truck is the far side of the lot. The near side is for the lumber delivery.
- The bakery asked for a board count and has not sent sizes. No quote until the sizes exist.
- A deposit check stays in the safe until the job it belongs to ships.
- The short hinge screw strips in soft maple. The long screw is the one in the cup.

Retain the old door card in the sleeve so we know what came down.
