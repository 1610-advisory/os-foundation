- A visitor who will not wear glasses does not walk the floor. That is a station rule.
- Register close is the usual close. A drawer off by more than $20 gets a slip, not a forced number.
- Crate corners reorder at 40. Pinebox Supply is the vendor. Lead time has been ten days.
- The pad from yesterday is still under the new pad. Look there before saying no one called.
- Plover Books needs a shelf quote. Measurements are still the customer's, not ours.
- The bakery asked for a board count and has not sent sizes. No quote until the sizes exist.
- Three price cards still show last year's type. The rest of the set can stay.
- Milltown Library's cart is a repair, not a new build, until the broken wheel is in the shop.

Customer numbers are customer numbers. The drawer is separate.

Cedar Route Nursery, quote I will send Tuesday if the count holds: four oak boxes, pickup, customer price $980. Cedar was short. I told the pad oak, not cedar. No delivery, their request.

The quote file and the letter do not go in the same sleeve. I moved the letter back.
