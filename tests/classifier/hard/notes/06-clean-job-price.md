# Fen Coast Inn, customer price

Monday 13 April. Harbor Mill. Fen Coast Inn is the signed rail and a maybe on two desks. The inn is a customer.

- Saltbox Bakery is a customer. Their order is not cut ahead of a signed job.
- Lowood Lumber's sticker on the oak still says the March lot, and the count on the sticker does not match the rack.
- Backup saw stays in use until the belt for the main saw is on the bench, not just on a truck.
- The nail test is the finisher's call on the day. Write the result on the board.
- The hotel site and the inn are different jobs. Do not share hardware between the two carts.
- Pinebox invoices go in the vendor tray. The bookkeeper takes that tray on Fridays.
- Parking for the truck is the far side of the lot. The near side is for the lumber delivery.
- If the finish room feels damp, the kiln goes on at 6 a.m. and the north window opens a finger.
