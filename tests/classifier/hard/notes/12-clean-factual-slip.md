# Late crates, customer email

Tuesday 21 April. Harbor Mill. Birch Lane Market got the crates two days late. They wrote. The shop already knows the truck waited on corners.

- Scrap oak goes in the scrap bin, not back on the blank count.
- Soft-close hinges for the inn job are a one-week lead from Pinebox Supply.
- Three price cards still show last year's type. The rest of the set can stay.
- If the finish room feels damp, the kiln goes on at 6 a.m. and the north window opens a finger.
- Milltown Library's cart is a repair, not a new build, until the broken wheel is in the shop.
- West Dock Co-op asked about a display table. Send questions, not a price, until the top size is known.
- Saltbox Bakery is a customer. Their order is not cut ahead of a signed job.
- The sample board leans on the north wall. It is not on the ship list.

What follows is what happened and what the customer wrote. Dates and a complaint. I am not adding a private read on anyone.

Facts on the card:
- corners landed Thursday, not Tuesday
- truck went Wednesday
- Birch Lane wanted Tuesday
- crates were complete when they left
- two days late against the day we had said
- the owner was on the dock Wednesday morning counting crates himself
- he said the date for the next one holds
- that is what he said

Pasted email from Birch Lane:

The crates arrived Wednesday. We needed them Tuesday for the market build. Two were scuffed on the outside corner. The inside was fine. We will pay the invoice because the crates are in use. Next time we need the day you say. Please ignore the phone message from our volunteer. She thought the order was missing. It was not missing. It was late.

Between us, the corners will not arrive before Thursday on the next Pinebox run if we order today. That is a vendor lead time. I am telling the floor lead so the card does not promise Tuesday again.

Transcript at the rack:

Floor lead: corners were the wait.
Me: yes.
Floor lead: scuffs are from the strap.
Me: add pads on the next load.
Floor lead: school dock is still 2.
Me: write it on the card tonight.

No one is in trouble in this note. The strap pads are a supply item, a few dollars, vendor tray if we buy a pack. Performance of the new pump is not why the truck was late. Payroll run is Friday, ordinary. Hire a subcontractor is unrelated.

Customer late fee does not apply backwards on a job we delivered late. Their invoice stands at the agreed customer price.

- Three price cards still show last year's type. The rest of the set can stay.
- Wet doors do not get stacked. The stickers leave a mark that the oil will not hide.
- The loading door sticks when the wind is from the west. Lift and then pull.
- Lowood delivers on their route, not on a called pickup, unless the board says a pickup was booked.
- Black pulls get reordered at ten. Hart and Coil is the vendor. The price is a material cost.
- Milltown Library's cart is a repair, not a new build, until the broken wheel is in the shop.
- The pack bench light flickers on the left tube. Leave it until Friday's electrician window.
- The bakery asked for a board count and has not sent sizes. No quote until the sizes exist.

Retain the scuff photo in the job sleeve in case they ask again. They said they will pay.
