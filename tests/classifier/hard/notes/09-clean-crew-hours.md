# Shop clock and saw rate

Thursday 16 April. Harbor Mill. The school job and the rail are both moving. This note is so the job cost stays honest.

- The pad from yesterday is still under the new pad. Look there before saying no one called.
- Phone messages go on the pad, with the time, even if the call felt short.
- The loading door sticks when the wind is from the west. Lift and then pull.
- Parking for the truck is the far side of the lot. The near side is for the lumber delivery.
- Oil cans reorder at six. Write the lot number on the board when a new can is opened.
- Sable Oil lots do not get mixed in one can. The new can stays sealed until the old one is empty.
- Harrow Street Hotel's sideboard sample ships only if the finish is dry to a fingernail.
- The front desk books the booth table. That is a calendar task.

Hours below are the shop clock on a job. The rate below is how fast the saw eats blanks. Neither one is a person's wage.

Crew hours on the school crates so far: 46 on the shop clock, across the week, written as a job total. The rate on the main saw, when the belt is on, is about 12 blanks an hour. On the backup saw the rate is slower, closer to 8, because the fence is fussy. Those rates are pieces per hour. They go on the job cost so we know if the customer price still covers the wood and the time.

No names on that table on purpose. The clock sheet for Friday's payroll run is a different paper, the one the bookkeeper already expects, totals by day.

Pasted from the floor lead's scrap:

backup saw rate is the problem not the people count. fence slips. I shimmed it. 8 an hour after the shim, was 6. school still ships Tuesday if corners hold. do not pull the inn maple for crates.

Customer price on the school quote, if they add 40 crates, is about $1,400. Vendor price on corners is still Pinebox. Pay the vendor invoice when the slip matches. Hire a subcontractor is not how we catch a saw rate.

Performance of the new pump stayed green during the crate run. The cloud did not come back.

- Customer checks go in the safe, not in the vendor tray.
- If the finish room feels damp, the kiln goes on at 6 a.m. and the north window opens a finger.
- Sixteen hinges are on the shelf. They are not to be borrowed for the crate order.
- Dust hose on the backup saw was taped last month. Look at the tape before the first cut.
- The blue bin is light. A spare box is on the mezzanine, left side, under the paper towels.
- Last time the card was blank the driver waited at a locked gate until 2:10.
- Two frames from the last shoot have a lamp in them. The photographer is a morning booking.
- The loading door sticks when the wind is from the west. Lift and then pull.

Fen Coast Inn's rail hours are the 18 above. Their signed price is unchanged.
