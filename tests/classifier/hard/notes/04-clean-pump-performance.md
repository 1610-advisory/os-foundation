# Dust line, pump performance

Thursday 9 April. Harbor Mill. Morning went to the dust line because the finisher said the cloud was back at the backup saw.

- The sample board leans on the north wall. It is not on the ship list.
- The marker on the whiteboard failed halfway through the ship list, so this note is the copy.
- Milltown Library's cart is a repair, not a new build, until the broken wheel is in the shop.
- Sandpaper 120 and 180 are down to two sleeves, which is enough for this week and not the next.
- West Dock Co-op asked about a display table. Send questions, not a price, until the top size is known.
- Customer checks go in the safe, not in the vendor tray.
- Maple for the rail is in the long rack, sticker out, so the next person does not recut it.
- If the finish room feels damp, the kiln goes on at 6 a.m. and the north window opens a finger.

Everything below about performance is the machine, the hose, or the finish schedule. Not a person.

Performance of the new pump:

The gauge sat in the green at 6:40 and in the yellow by 8:05, then green again after the hose was reseated. Performance dropped when the tape joint folded in. Performance came back when the joint was cut and reclamped. I wrote the times on the housing in pencil.

What I tried:
- reseat the hose at the saw
- reseat the hose at the pump
- empty the drum. it was a third full, not the cause
- listen for the bearing. no new sound
- leave the housing closed
- run one board. cloud gone
- run ten boards. cloud still gone

Pasted from the pad the finisher left:

pump performance bad at 8, ok at 8:20. do not open it. tell Lowood only if the yellow comes back tomorrow. the old pump is in the mezzanine if this one dies. old pump performance was worse, which is why it is in the mezzanine.

A customer, Red Kettle Cafe, called during the test and I let it ring. Their unsigned bread-board note can wait a day. Pay the vendor invoice for the clamps if Hart and Coil's slip shows up. Hire a subcontractor is still not a plan for this. The pump is ours.

Payroll run is Friday. I will not still be on this housing then, unless the yellow band returns.

Fire the kiln only if the room is damp this afternoon. The pump test warmed the room. Crack the window if the oil skins too fast.
