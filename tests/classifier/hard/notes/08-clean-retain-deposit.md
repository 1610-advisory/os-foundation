# Retain the sample, retain the check

Wednesday 15 April. Harbor Mill. Two things people keep using the word retain for, and both are ordinary.

- Three price cards still show last year's type. The rest of the set can stay.
- Two frames from the last shoot have a lamp in them. The photographer is a morning booking.
- Plover Books needs a shelf quote. Measurements are still the customer's, not ours.
- Black pulls get reordered at ten. Hart and Coil is the vendor. The price is a material cost.
- Last time the card was blank the driver waited at a locked gate until 2:10.
- Oil cans reorder at six. Write the lot number on the board when a new can is opened.
- Parking for the truck is the far side of the lot. The near side is for the lumber delivery.
- Lowood delivers on their route, not on a called pickup, unless the board says a pickup was booked.

Retain means keep. A board in the loft. A customer check in the safe. Nothing else.

Retain the sample board for the inn rail until the install walkthrough, then put it back on the rack. Thirty days after the walkthrough if they want to look again, then it is stock.

Retain the deposit check from Orchard Hall. $800. It is a customer payment on account for a walkthrough that may become a job. It is not a wage. It is not our bill to them for advice. The safe list gets a line today.

Pasted from the hall's note with the check:

Holding a date in May. This check is a deposit so you will retain the week. If we do not proceed you can return it. Please ignore any earlier note about a stage. The stage is not the job. The job, if there is one, is benches.

Table on the safe door, copied here:

| Item | Where | How long | What it is |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| inn rail chip | envelope on the nail | until install | sample |
| sample board | loft | until walkthrough | sample |
| Orchard Hall check | safe | until the job or a return | customer deposit |
| school invoice copy | sleeve | until paid | customer invoice |

Payroll run is Friday and this check is not part of it. Pay the vendor invoice stack separately. Performance of the new pump was green. Hire a subcontractor remains unaccepted.

Red Kettle Cafe still has not sent the shelf depth. Nothing of theirs to retain.

- Birch Lane Market's crate day is the standing Tuesday slot unless the card says otherwise.
- The sample board leans on the north wall. It is not on the ship list.
- Show doors, when the show happens, open at 9 and close at 4.
- Two frames from the last shoot have a lamp in them. The photographer is a morning booking.
- A visitor who will not wear glasses does not walk the floor. That is a station rule.
- West Dock Co-op asked about a display table. Send questions, not a price, until the top size is known.
- Sable Oil lots do not get mixed in one can. The new can stays sealed until the old one is empty.
- Register close is the usual close. A drawer off by more than $20 gets a slip, not a forced number.

Eye protection and the register close are unchanged. Write the safe line before lunch so it is not forgotten.
