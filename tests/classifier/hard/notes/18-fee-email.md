# Bookkeeper email, buried

Thursday 30 April. Harbor Mill. Morning was the nursery quote draft and a tangled vendor slip. I am pasting mail so I do not retype numbers wrong.

- The blue bin is light. A spare box is on the mezzanine, left side, under the paper towels.
- If the finish room feels damp, the kiln goes on at 6 a.m. and the north window opens a finger.
- Soft-close hinges for the inn job are a one-week lead from Pinebox Supply.
- A visitor who will not wear glasses does not walk the floor. That is a station rule.
- The nail test is the finisher's call on the day. Write the result on the board.
- A deposit check stays in the safe until the job it belongs to ships.
- The marker on the whiteboard failed halfway through the ship list, so this note is the copy.
- The short hinge screw strips in soft maple. The long screw is the one in the cup.

The mail is mostly the tray. One sentence in it is our letter.

Nursery draft, customer price, not sent: four cedar boxes if the rack has cedar, otherwise oak, pickup, no delivery. I will price it when I count the cedar. Cedar Route Nursery picks up. Their words.

Vendor tangle: Hart and Coil slip appeared under the pad, not in the tray. Pay the vendor invoice now that the slip exists. $48, black pulls.

Pasted email from the bookkeeper:

Subject: tray and the letter

Pinebox 441 matches. Lowood matches. Sable matches. Hart and Coil matches if you found the slip. School 1904 is their invoice to pay, day 30 is next week, no late fee today. Inn rail is a customer price already signed. Cafe is not a job. On the other paper, the one in the drawer with our letter, confirming we are at 12 hours a month at the usual rate. I will not draft a new bill. Reply if that changed. Clock sheet still Friday for payroll run.

I replied only "slip is found, letter unchanged."
