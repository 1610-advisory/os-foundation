# Door card and a letter

Thursday 21 May. Harbor Mill. The open role card is still on the door. Separately, a letter went out. I am writing both so I do not confuse them next week.

- Three price cards still show last year's type. The rest of the set can stay.
- Lowood delivers on their route, not on a called pickup, unless the board says a pickup was booked.
- If a customer calls about a date, write the call on the card. Do not promise from memory.
- The sample board leans on the north wall. It is not on the ship list.
- Oil cans reorder at six. Write the lot number on the board when a new can is opened.
- The nail test is the finisher's call on the day. Write the result on the board.
- Saltbox Bakery is a customer. Their order is not cut ahead of a signed job.
- The printer gets those three cards only. Not a reorder of the whole stack.

The door card is still an open role with no name. The letter is a decision.

Door card, unchanged in meaning:

Finisher wanted. Oil and samples. Ask at the office. No pay figure on the card. No name on the card.

The letter is not the card.

The offer to Ren Moss went out this morning. She starts if she signs.

I sent it from the office computer before the floor meeting. I did not announce it. If she signs, the door card comes down that day. If she does not, the card stays and this sentence was early. I am not interviewing anyone else in this note.

Shop around that, so Thursday is not only the letter:
- pump gauge green at 6:45. performance of the new pump, fine
- Birch Lane next Tuesday
- school still thinking
- nursery still thinking
- cafe depth still missing. Red Kettle Cafe
- payroll run Friday, current sheet only, the letter is not on it
- pay the vendor invoice stack as usual
- hire a subcontractor, Bramble, still unused and not a substitute for the letter
- Quill and Brass Print, cards
- Lumen Wick Antiques, no date
- Cedar Route Nursery, waiting
- Fen Coast Inn, no callback

Transcript of what I told the floor lead, which omitted the letter:

Me: list at 7, same as ever.
Floor lead: truck Tuesday.
Me: yes.
Floor lead: door card stays.
Me: yes.

I will not add Ren's name to the door. The door stays nameless until there is a start date.

- Backup saw stays in use until the belt for the main saw is on the bench, not just on a truck.
- The hotel site and the inn are different jobs. Do not share hardware between the two carts.
- The printer gets those three cards only. Not a reorder of the whole stack.
- If a customer calls about a date, write the call on the card. Do not promise from memory.
- Fuel stop stays the old station. The truck does not fit the canopy at the highway one.
- Driver copies the card onto the clipboard. The office copy stays on the nail.
- Eye protection stays on at every saw. Spares live in the blue bin by the north door.
- Harrow Street Hotel's sideboard sample ships only if the finish is dry to a fingernail.

The owner knows I was going to send a letter. He asked for it last week in one sentence. This note records that it went.
