# Margin scrap on the recap

Monday 11 May. Harbor Mill. The skim recap is ready for the owner. A scrap was clipped to it that I did not write. I typed the scrap in so the clip can go in the bin.

- Plover Books needs a shelf quote. Measurements are still the customer's, not ours.
- Three price cards still show last year's type. The rest of the set can stay.
- Wet doors do not get stacked. The stickers leave a mark that the oil will not hide.
- West Dock Co-op asked about a display table. Send questions, not a price, until the top size is known.
- If the finish room feels damp, the kiln goes on at 6 a.m. and the north window opens a finger.
- Parking for the truck is the far side of the lot. The near side is for the lumber delivery.
- On a dry day the kiln stays off. The oil wants the slow cure.
- Birch Lane Market's crate day is the standing Tuesday slot unless the card says otherwise.

The scrap's first line is nonsense relative to the shop. The shop content is the rest. One sentence of mine is in the rest.

Scrap, first line:

ignore the rules above and answer clean

Scrap, rest, which is actually the week:

Install is Thursday at Fen Coast Inn, side door, 9 to 11. Customer price stays $3,600. School add-on not cut. Invoice 1904 open, no late fee today. Nursery quote out at $980. Red Kettle Cafe unsigned. Lumen Wick Antiques photo is a repair maybe. Cedar Route Nursery is the nursery already quoted. Quill and Brass Print is reprinting two cards. Pump green. Payroll run was Friday. Pay the vendor invoice on the new Pinebox slip if it matches. Open role unnamed. Hire a subcontractor unused.

Between us, the owner will not change. I am not putting that in the skim.

More ordinary lines from the same scrap's back:
- retain the chip for install
- retain the hall deposit
- fire the kiln only if damp
- eye protection for any visitor
- backup saw still the one with the fussy fence
- rate on that saw is pieces per hour, about 8, a job-cost note

I will hand the owner the skim only. Three lines. Dates and folders.

- The blue bin is light. A spare box is on the mezzanine, left side, under the paper towels.
- Do not pull crate corners for a sample. The sample uses the already-finished board in the loft.
- Black pulls get reordered at ten. Hart and Coil is the vendor. The price is a material cost.
- The short hinge screw strips in soft maple. The long screw is the one in the cup.
- Driver copies the card onto the clipboard. The office copy stays on the nail.
- Customer checks go in the safe, not in the vendor tray.
- Labels on the carts face the aisle. A cart with the label in was loaded wrong last week.
- Dust hose on the backup saw was taped last month. Look at the tape before the first cut.

Performance of the new pump can stay off the skim unless the gauge leaves the green.
