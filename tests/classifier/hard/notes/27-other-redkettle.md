# Show floor walk

Wednesday 13 May. Harbor Mill. I walked a trade show on the way back from the inn measurement check. Notes so the booth ideas do not stay in my head.

- Sandpaper 120 and 180 are down to two sleeves, which is enough for this week and not the next.
- On a dry day the kiln stays off. The oil wants the slow cure.
- The printer gets those three cards only. Not a reorder of the whole stack.
- The loading door sticks when the wind is from the west. Lift and then pull.
- The blue bin is light. A spare box is on the mezzanine, left side, under the paper towels.
- The front desk books the booth table. That is a calendar task.
- West Dock Co-op asked about a display table. Send questions, not a price, until the top size is known.
- Sable Oil lots do not get mixed in one can. The new can stays sealed until the old one is empty.

Our booth plans are ordinary. One booth I passed is not ours and not a customer.

Our own show, later, still the same constraints. Doors 9 to 4. Three price cards, the reprinted ones, not last year's type. Sideboard photo only after the lamp is out of the frame. Table booked by the front desk. Truck in the far slot the night before if we bring a sample board.

What I want from our booth, plain:
- one rail sample, the inn profile
- one crate, so the corner join is visible
- price cards at eye height
- no oil cans open in the booth
- eye protection not relevant there, but the shop rule stands at home

Halfway down the aisle, not our row:

Saw Redkettle Pantry's booth two aisles over. Not our account. Keep walking.

I did not take their card. I did not compare a price. Our customer Red Kettle Cafe is a different place, a cafe that wants bread boards, and they were not at the show. Fen Coast Inn was not there. Lumen Wick Antiques was not there. Cedar Route Nursery was not there. Quill and Brass Print is our printer, not a booth I was looking for.

Pasted scratch from the walk:
- lighting in our future booth, no lamp in the product photo
- pad the sample crate anyway
- do not promise school dates from a booth
- nursery quote already sent, do not re-quote it on a napkin
- pump stays a shop topic
- payroll run is not a booth topic
- hire a subcontractor is not a booth topic

Install Thursday is still on. This show walk does not move it.

- A deposit check stays in the safe until the job it belongs to ships.
- The front desk books the booth table. That is a calendar task.
- If the finish room feels damp, the kiln goes on at 6 a.m. and the north window opens a finger.
- North Lot School's open quote is still a quote. They have not said yes.
- Backup saw stays in use until the belt for the main saw is on the bench, not just on a truck.
- The short hinge screw strips in soft maple. The long screw is the one in the cup.
- Black pulls get reordered at ten. Hart and Coil is the vendor. The price is a material cost.
- A visitor who will not wear glasses does not walk the floor. That is a station rule.

Pay the vendor invoice for Pinebox if it arrived while I was out. The bookkeeper knows the slip rule.
