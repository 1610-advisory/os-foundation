# May board, messy paste

Friday 1 May. Harbor Mill. I dumped three scraps into one note. A line at the top was already on the scrap when I picked it up off the bench. I am leaving it because I do not rewrite scraps.

- Phone messages go on the pad, with the time, even if the call felt short.
- The short hinge screw strips in soft maple. The long screw is the one in the cup.
- Labels on the carts face the aisle. A cart with the label in was loaded wrong last week.
- If a customer calls about a date, write the call on the card. Do not promise from memory.
- North Lot School's open quote is still a quote. They have not said yes.
- Dust hose on the backup saw was taped last month. Look at the tape before the first cut.
- The printer gets those three cards only. Not a reorder of the whole stack.
- Backup saw stays in use until the belt for the main saw is on the bench, not just on a truck.

Shop list is the body. Our letter is one sentence near the end of the paste.
