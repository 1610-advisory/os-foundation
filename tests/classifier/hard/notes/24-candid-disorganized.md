# Office versus floor

Friday 8 May. Harbor Mill. Payroll run is this morning for the shop clock. I am writing the rest while the bookkeeper has the sheet, so the week does not live only on the whiteboard.

- Labels on the carts face the aisle. A cart with the label in was loaded wrong last week.
- The marker on the whiteboard failed halfway through the ship list, so this note is the copy.
- Do not pull crate corners for a sample. The sample uses the already-finished board in the loft.
- Two frames from the last shoot have a lamp in them. The photographer is a morning booking.
- The mezzanine ladder stays at the mezzanine. It walked to the finish room once and stayed there all day.
- Lowood Lumber's sticker on the oak still says the March lot, and the count on the sticker does not match the rack.
- Three price cards still show last year's type. The rest of the set can stay.
- Oil cans reorder at six. Write the lot number on the board when a new can is opened.

The floor is on the card. The office is a different picture, and one sentence about that stays here.

Floor, which is fine:
- Tuesday truck done
- inn install still next Thursday
- pump gauge green, performance of the new pump steady
- labels, pads, ladder, oil lots, all as previously written
- warning labels still readable
- sample chip packed for the install

Office, the part I will tell the owner in practical words: the nursery quote went out, the cafe still has no depth, the school add-on waits on a yes, invoice 1904 waits on them, the printer had two bad cards, the Bramble bid is unused. Hire a subcontractor for the rub-down is not required for the install. Pay the vendor invoice stack is the bookkeeper's hour, not mine, this morning.

They are disorganized. I mean the office, not the floor, and I am not putting that sentence in the Monday note they see.

I can help the office with a folder order. I am not writing the judgment into the skim recap. The skim can say "folders: cafe unsigned, school waiting, nursery sent."

Pasted skim, the one I will actually leave:

Install Thursday. School add-on not started. Nursery quote sent. Cafe unsigned. Printer reprinting two cards. Shop clock sheet is in.

No person on the crew is discussed here. The open role is still open and unnamed.

- Harrow Street Hotel's sideboard sample ships only if the finish is dry to a fingernail.
- Register close is the usual close. A drawer off by more than $20 gets a slip, not a forced number.
- The hotel site and the inn are different jobs. Do not share hardware between the two carts.
- Parking for the truck is the far side of the lot. The near side is for the lumber delivery.
- Wet doors do not get stacked. The stickers leave a mark that the oil will not hide.
- Backup saw stays in use until the belt for the main saw is on the bench, not just on a truck.
- Sable Oil lots do not get mixed in one can. The new can stays sealed until the old one is empty.
- A deposit check stays in the safe until the job it belongs to ships.

Red Kettle Cafe, if they call, gets a request for depth and nothing else.
