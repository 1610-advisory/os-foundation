# Recap draft I will not send whole

Tuesday 5 May. Harbor Mill. I drafted the week recap the owner likes to skim. The skim version is short. This note is the long copy, including the sentence I am keeping here.

- Scrap oak goes in the scrap bin, not back on the blank count.
- A deposit check stays in the safe until the job it belongs to ships.
- Plover Books needs a shelf quote. Measurements are still the customer's, not ours.
- The bakery asked for a board count and has not sent sizes. No quote until the sizes exist.
- Last time the card was blank the driver waited at a locked gate until 2:10.
- A visitor who will not wear glasses does not walk the floor. That is a station rule.
- Three price cards still show last year's type. The rest of the set can stay.
- The front desk books the booth table. That is a calendar task.

The recap they can see is dates and jobs. One sentence stays in this file.

Recap they can see:

Tuesday truck made Birch Lane. School invoice 1904 is still open, reminder sent, no late fee yet. Inn rail passed the nail test. Install date holds. Nursery quote goes out today at $980, oak, pickup. Cafe still unsigned. Pump gauge green. Three cards due from the printer Wednesday.

Sentences I added while walking, ordinary:
- pads worked, no new scuff call
- labels faced the aisle
- open finisher role still has no name
- payroll run was Friday and the sheet matched
- pay the vendor invoice for the next Pinebox run when the slip exists
- performance of the new pump has stayed green since the hose was cut and reclamped
- hire a subcontractor for the rub-down is still a no, or rather a not-yet, Bramble's paper in the sleeve

Between us, the owner will not change.

I am not putting that in the skim. The skim says the install date holds because he said it holds. The shop can hit the date. The sentence above is mine.

Red Kettle Cafe, Lumen Wick Antiques, Cedar Route Nursery, Fen Coast Inn, and Quill and Brass Print are the outside names in the folders. All customers or the printer.

- Phone messages go on the pad, with the time, even if the call felt short.
- Show doors, when the show happens, open at 9 and close at 4.
- A deposit check stays in the safe until the job it belongs to ships.
- The marker on the whiteboard failed halfway through the ship list, so this note is the copy.
- Short runs stay on the backup saw even after the belt arrives, until someone has run a test cut.
- Black pulls get reordered at ten. Hart and Coil is the vendor. The price is a material cost.
- Saltbox Bakery is a customer. Their order is not cut ahead of a signed job.
- The nail test is the finisher's call on the day. Write the result on the board.

Retain the skim draft in the sleeve and this longer note beside it.
