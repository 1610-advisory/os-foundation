# Doorway transcript, 28 April

Tuesday 28 April. Harbor Mill. The bookkeeper stood in the doorway with the tray. We talked through paper. I am writing the talk because the tray moves on Friday and the words will not.

- Plover Books needs a shelf quote. Measurements are still the customer's, not ours.
- A visitor who will not wear glasses does not walk the floor. That is a station rule.
- Harrow Street Hotel's sideboard sample ships only if the finish is dry to a fingernail.
- Customer checks go in the safe, not in the vendor tray.
- The marker on the whiteboard failed halfway through the ship list, so this note is the copy.
- Black pulls get reordered at ten. Hart and Coil is the vendor. The price is a material cost.
- The school dock closes at 2. The card has to say so the day before, not the morning of.
- Dust hose on the backup saw was taped last month. Look at the tape before the first cut.

Shop paper first. Our own letter at the end of the middle, one exchange.

Transcript.

Bookkeeper: Pinebox slip is clipped. Pay it.
Me: yes. Vendor bill.
Bookkeeper: Lowood is already in.
Me: good.
Bookkeeper: school invoice 1904 is still open. Their late fee, if any, waits for day 30.
Me: send the reminder, no fee today.
Bookkeeper: inn deposit is not a thing. They are on a signed price.
Me: $3,600 customer price. Desks are not real.
Bookkeeper: cafe folder is thin.
Me: unsigned. Red Kettle Cafe. Depth missing.
Bookkeeper: nursery.
Me: quote, not a bill.
Bookkeeper: clock sheet Friday.
Me: payroll run is Friday. Shop clock.
Bookkeeper: and the drawer letter, ours.
Me: on our letter we are at twelve hours a month at the usual rate.
Bookkeeper: I will not start a new bill.
Me: correct. Next item is the dock.
Bookkeeper: school dock closes at 2.
Me: write it on the card.

That was the doorway.

After, I checked the pump gauge. Green. Performance of the machine, fine. Hire a subcontractor for the rub-down still sits as Bramble's bid, not accepted, a vendor price of $2,400 if we ever say yes.

No names of crew in the transcript on purpose. The floor lead posts the list at 7 as usual.

- Fuel stop stays the old station. The truck does not fit the canopy at the highway one.
- Soft-close hinges for the inn job are a one-week lead from Pinebox Supply.
- Eye protection stays on at every saw. Spares live in the blue bin by the north door.
- Pinebox invoices go in the vendor tray. The bookkeeper takes that tray on Fridays.
- Backup saw stays in use until the belt for the main saw is on the bench, not just on a truck.
- Sandpaper 120 and 180 are down to two sleeves, which is enough for this week and not the next.
- The system said eleven more oak blanks than the hand count, so the hand count is the one on the board.
- Black pulls get reordered at ten. Hart and Coil is the vendor. The price is a material cost.

Retain the nursery sizes on the pad until the quote exists.
