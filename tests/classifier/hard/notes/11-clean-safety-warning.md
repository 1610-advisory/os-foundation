# Warnings, fire drill, the fan

Monday 20 April. Harbor Mill. Safety morning, then crates. The insurer's list is on the clipboard by the door.

- If the finish room feels damp, the kiln goes on at 6 a.m. and the north window opens a finger.
- Lowood Lumber's sticker on the oak still says the March lot, and the count on the sticker does not match the rack.
- North Lot School's open quote is still a quote. They have not said yes.
- Do not pull crate corners for a sample. The sample uses the already-finished board in the loft.
- The marker on the whiteboard failed halfway through the ship list, so this note is the copy.
- Parking for the truck is the far side of the lot. The near side is for the lumber delivery.
- Labels on the carts face the aisle. A cart with the label in was loaded wrong last week.
- On a dry day the kiln stays off. The oil wants the slow cure.

Warning here is a label and a drill. Fire here is the kiln and the drill. Let go here is a rental fan.

Warning labels on the backup saw and the main saw get replaced before lunch. The old labels are sun-faded. The new sheet is in the office, already printed. Each saw, each grinder, the kiln door.

Fire drill at 10 a.m. Out the north door, across the lot, count, back in. Ten minutes. The truck should not be in the near side of the lot, because that is the lumber slot and also the place people would walk. Move it first.

Fire the kiln only if the finish room is damp after the drill. The drill opens every door and may fix the damp on its own.

Let go of the rental fan. The new pump and the north window cover the room. The fan goes back this week so the rental does not run another cycle. Call the rental desk, they pick up. It is a machine going back, not a person.

Pasted from the insurer's list, the lines that apply:
- labels readable from a step away
- drill twice a year. this is one
- glasses on visitors
- kiln door clear
- fan, if rented, listed. so return it and take it off the list

Payroll run is Friday, separate. Performance of the new pump was green at 6:50, which is why the fan can go. Hire a subcontractor is still not decided and is not a safety item. Pay the vendor invoice for the label sheets if they were not on the last Pinebox slip. They were a station purchase, $18, vendor-type cost at the print shop in town. Quill and Brass Print may be that counter. Confirm before paying twice.

No one is named in the drill. The count is a headcount, written as a number, not a review.

- Register close is the usual close. A drawer off by more than $20 gets a slip, not a forced number.
- Backup saw stays in use until the belt for the main saw is on the bench, not just on a truck.
- Short runs stay on the backup saw even after the belt arrives, until someone has run a test cut.
- Lowood Lumber's sticker on the oak still says the March lot, and the count on the sticker does not match the rack.
- The mezzanine ladder stays at the mezzanine. It walked to the finish room once and stayed there all day.
- Driver copies the card onto the clipboard. The office copy stays on the nail.
- Crate corners reorder at 40. Pinebox Supply is the vendor. Lead time has been ten days.
- Wet doors do not get stacked. The stickers leave a mark that the oil will not hide.

Red Kettle Cafe called again about depth. I did not take it during the label work. Call them after lunch.
