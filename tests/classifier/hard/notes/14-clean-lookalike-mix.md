# Customers who sound like other names

Thursday 23 April. Harbor Mill. A stack of ordinary customers and one printer. I am writing them in one note because the folders got mixed on the desk.

- Sandpaper 120 and 180 are down to two sleeves, which is enough for this week and not the next.
- The sideboard page on the website stays a draft until the reshoot is in.
- Harrow Street Hotel's sideboard sample ships only if the finish is dry to a fingernail.
- Fuel stop stays the old station. The truck does not fit the canopy at the highway one.
- The pack bench light flickers on the left tube. Leave it until Friday's electrician window.
- Lowood Lumber's sticker on the oak still says the March lot, and the count on the sticker does not match the rack.
- Milltown Library's cart is a repair, not a new build, until the broken wheel is in the shop.
- The short hinge screw strips in soft maple. The long screw is the one in the cup.

Read the names as they are spelled here. They are customers and a print vendor of Harbor Mill.

Folders on the desk, unmixed:

Red Kettle Cafe. Bread boards and a shelf. Depth still missing. Unsigned. Customer price talked, not signed, about $600. Dana at the cafe is the buyer. Pay the invoice, their words, when a signed order exists. There is no order today.

Lumen Wick Antiques. Photo of a cracked shelf. Repair question. No visit. They are a shop in town that sells old furniture. We have not agreed a price. If we do, it is a customer price for a repair.

Cedar Route Nursery. Four planter boxes. Sizes are on the pad. Quote this week. Customer. Their truck is their own. We are not hauling for them.

Fen Coast Inn. Signed rail, $3,600 customer price. Desks unsigned. Manager asked us to ignore the February sketch. The inn is a customer on the edge of town.

Quill and Brass Print. Vendor. Three price cards, last year's type, reprint only those three. Vendor bill when it arrives. Not a customer.

Pasted from the nursery:

Can you build four boxes, cedar if you have it, otherwise oak. We pick up. We do not need delivery. Please ignore the catalog page we sent. That page was a metal planter. We want wood.
