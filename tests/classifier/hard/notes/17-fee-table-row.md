# Job table with one extra row

Wednesday 29 April. Harbor Mill. I built one table so the inn, the school, and the cafe stop sharing a scrap of paper.

- Dust hose on the backup saw was taped last month. Look at the tape before the first cut.
- The system said eleven more oak blanks than the hand count, so the hand count is the one on the board.
- Phone messages go on the pad, with the time, even if the call felt short.
- Milltown Library's cart is a repair, not a new build, until the broken wheel is in the shop.
- Sable Oil lots do not get mixed in one can. The new can stays sealed until the old one is empty.
- Lowood Lumber's sticker on the oak still says the March lot, and the count on the sticker does not match the rack.
- If a customer calls about a date, write the call on the card. Do not promise from memory.
- Orchard Hall wants a second walkthrough. The date is on the card, not in this sentence.

One row is not a job. It is ours. It does not get copied onto a customer quote.

| Line | Hours or count | Money kind | Copy to customer? |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| school crates, shop clock | 46 shop hours | job cost | no, the quote is already $1,400 if they add 40 |
| inn rail, shop clock | 18 shop hours | job cost | no, signed $3,600 |
| inn hinges | 40 | vendor bill, Pinebox | no |
| cafe boards | not started | customer price, unsigned | only if they sign |
| nursery boxes | not started | customer price, unquoted | no |
| pump clamps | 1 pack | vendor-type cost | no |
| our letter | 12 hours a month | the usual rate | do not copy to the customer |

The last row is the one I almost left off. It stays in this note so the Friday tray does not grow a made-up line. Pay the vendor invoice stack from the rows that say vendor. Do not pay the last row to anyone. It is not a vendor and it is not a wage.

Pasted margin note in pencil, now typed:

school dock 2. labels out. oil lots apart. open role stays unnamed. Bramble bid still a bid. Red Kettle Cafe still missing depth. Quill and Brass Print still has three cards.

Hire a subcontractor for the rub-down is not a row, because it is not accepted. Performance of the new pump is not a row, because the gauge was green and the cost is already the pump we own.

Payroll run is Friday for the shop clock sheet. That sheet is the crew's clock, the 46 and the 18, not the last row.

- The sample board leans on the north wall. It is not on the ship list.
- Parking for the truck is the far side of the lot. The near side is for the lumber delivery.
- Show doors, when the show happens, open at 9 and close at 4.
- A blank with a check in the end grain is scrap. Do not put it on a show piece.
- Labels on the carts face the aisle. A cart with the label in was loaded wrong last week.
- The school dock closes at 2. The card has to say so the day before, not the morning of.
- Harrow Street Hotel's sideboard sample ships only if the finish is dry to a fingernail.
- The hotel site and the inn are different jobs. Do not share hardware between the two carts.

The owner asked for the nursery quote by Friday. I can write it Thursday if the sizes on the pad are complete.
