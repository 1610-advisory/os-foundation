# Seasonal close, shop plan

Wednesday 20 May. Harbor Mill. The seasonal crate push ends after the first June market. I am writing what the floor looks like after that, mostly schedules.

- Driver copies the card onto the clipboard. The office copy stays on the nail.
- The sample board leans on the north wall. It is not on the ship list.
- On a dry day the kiln stays off. The oil wants the slow cure.
- Parking for the truck is the far side of the lot. The near side is for the lumber delivery.
- Eye protection stays on at every saw. Spares live in the blue bin by the north door.
- The system said eleven more oak blanks than the hand count, so the hand count is the one on the board.
- The hotel site and the inn are different jobs. Do not share hardware between the two carts.
- Wet doors do not get stacked. The stickers leave a mark that the oil will not hide.

One decision is a person, and it is one sentence.

After the June market:
- Tuesday truck may drop to every other week
- school add-on, if yes, is the last crate burst
- inn has no phase two unless they sign desks
- nursery boxes are a small job
- finish room goes back to samples and repairs
- photographer crop can happen any morning
- open finisher role may stay open or may come down. not decided. no name on it
- rental fan stays returned
- warning labels stay

We are not bringing Ellis Dodd back after the seasonal close.

Ellis has been the extra hands on crate days. The June market is the last of those days in this note. I will tell him in the office, not on the floor, and not in the 7:10 meeting. I am not putting the sentence on the board.

Other decisions that are not about him:
- pay the vendor invoice for a last corner run only if the school says yes
- customer price on that add-on stays about $1,400
- payroll run continues Fridays for whoever is on the clock
- performance of the new pump, keep the gauge check
- hire a subcontractor for anything seasonal, no
- Red Kettle Cafe, still not a reason to add hands
- Lumen Wick Antiques, repair, no

Pasted half lines:
- pads back to the shelf after the last market
- labels still face out until then
- oil lots still apart
- Quill and Brass Print cards, if they ever match, go in the booth box

Fen Coast Inn install is already done and does not need Ellis.

- The school dock closes at 2. The card has to say so the day before, not the morning of.
- Pinebox invoices go in the vendor tray. The bookkeeper takes that tray on Fridays.
- Birch Lane Market's crate day is the standing Tuesday slot unless the card says otherwise.
- Saltbox Bakery is a customer. Their order is not cut ahead of a signed job.
- Soft-close hinges for the inn job are a one-week lead from Pinebox Supply.
- The loading door sticks when the wind is from the west. Lift and then pull.
- Sandpaper 120 and 180 are down to two sleeves, which is enough for this week and not the next.
- The short hinge screw strips in soft maple. The long screw is the one in the cup.

Retain the seasonal card until the June market, then rewrite it without the extra hands.
