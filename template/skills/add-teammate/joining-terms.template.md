# Joining terms — {{COMPANY_NAME}}

Team level only. A solo company has no teammates, so these terms do not apply yet.

`add-teammate` shows you this page before you get access. You say yes to it once, when you join. Nothing is asked of you on the day you leave.

## What you get

- A copy of the company folder and of each area your account can read, on your computer.
- Your own private workspace. Only you, your own AI and the admin can read it.
- Read access means you may keep a copy while you work here. Keep it in one place: not in a synced folder (iCloud Drive, Dropbox, OneDrive, Google Drive).

## The delete promise

When you leave, the company removes your access. Then:

1. **Updates stop at once.** Your sign-in no longer reaches the company repos.
2. **A helper deletes the company folder.** `add-teammate` installs a small helper on your computer. It deletes the company folder when it gets the leaving signal.
3. **You delete the copies outside the folder.** The helper cannot find them. See the list below.
4. **Managed computers:** if the company manages your computer, it may also wipe the folder that way.

## What counts as the leaving signal

- **With the company app:** the app tells the helper that your access is gone.
- **Without the app:** the helper counts days. A day counts only when all three are true:
  - the helper ran that day,
  - your sign-in works,
  - the company repo says "no access" to you.
- After 14 counted days, the helper deletes the folder. It warns you on your computer first.

These do **not** count:

- days when your computer is off or offline,
- days when your sign-in has expired (the helper only says "sign in again").

So leave alone never deletes anything: holiday, parental leave, sick leave or a long trip. Only the company removing your access does.

## If it ever deletes by mistake

Everything you pushed is safe on the company's git host: the company repo, your areas and your workspace. Run the join step of `add-teammate` again to get it all back. It takes minutes. You lose only work that never reached the host: at most the day before your access stopped, plus any files the nightly check held.

## Copies outside the folder

You agree to delete these yourself when you leave:

- **Agent transcripts and AI memory.** Your AI apps keep chat history, memory and session transcripts in their own folders or in your vendor account. The helper does not touch them.
- **Backups.** Time Machine, cloud backups, or any other backup of your computer.
- **Hand copies.** Files you downloaded, exported, emailed, printed or pasted somewhere else.

The company removes your seat at each AI vendor. What a vendor keeps after that is the vendor's rule, not the company's.

## What the company keeps

- Your notes and action logs in the areas. They are company knowledge.
- Your workspace, archived. Only the admin can read it. The admin may turn on deletion.
- Your page in `people/`, marked with the day you left.

## Outside parties

If you work here for an outside party (an advisory firm, a bookkeeper, an agency): when the work ends, you delete every copy of company data, as above. You may keep anonymized notes on methods: no names, numbers or anything that identifies the company. A special agreement with the company can say otherwise.

## Where copies sit

`privacy.md` lists every place a copy of the company's data sits, who can read it, and how it is removed.
